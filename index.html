Key Findings from Behavioral Analysis
◆
Screening score is the strongest predictor: Subjects scoring 7 or above on the 10-question behavioral screen test showed ASD positive rates exceeding 85%, making it a highly reliable pre-diagnostic tool.
▲
Questions A1, A5 & A8 are most discriminative: These three behavioral questions — related to social attention, pattern recognition, and contextual understanding — contributed the highest individual predictive signal.
●
Gender gap observed: Male subjects represent 62% of ASD positive cases, consistent with wider clinical literature showing higher ASD prevalence in males (4:1 ratio).
✓
Family history is a significant factor: Subjects with a family history of ASD showed a 2.3x higher likelihood of screening positive, highlighting the role of genetic predisposition.
